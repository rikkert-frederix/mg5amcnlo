\subsection{Complete flavour sum and numerical precision}

The main on-shell campaign comprises
$2\times8\times6\times5=480$ integrations: two charges, eight ordered
flavour assignments, six prescriptions and five independent trainings.
All archived complete-run vectors reconstruct the original histograms.
The audit finds 59,629 distinct stage-initialization pairs, with no
cross-run overlap. No run or integration split was excluded from the main
ensemble. All 81 scale points and 101 PDF members enter the reductions.

Table~\ref{tab:mainrates} gives the resulting rates and acceptances.
For the nominal one-b and two-b selections, the S and $\Pi$ rate errors
range from 0.26\% to 0.49\%. These are measured errors from the independent
run ensembles, rather than the requested 1.5\% accuracy of an individual
integration. They do not include scale or PDF uncertainty.

For differential comparisons we merge complete run vectors before taking
ratios or estimating errors. One common draft bin plan is applied to all
charges, prescriptions and cut choices. It retains the original ranges,
empty bins and separate 140--150 and 150--160 GeV minimax bins around the
Born endpoint. The bin boundaries were specified before inspecting the
full-flavour shape shifts. The original finer vectors remain archived.
Precision is not uniform: in the W$^+$ minimax spectrum, only five of
twelve populated bins reach a 2\% normalized-shape error for each of S
and $\Pi$. In contrast, all ten W$^+$ same-sign azimuthal bins meet that
target for both prescriptions. The figures retain the unresolved tails
with their measured errors; these are not final publication bins.

\subsection{Fiducial rates and acceptance migration}

The product-to-strict ratios at the nominal scales are
\begin{align}
 \left.\frac{\Pi}{S}\right|_{\geq1b}
 &=\begin{cases}
 0.9965\pm0.0044,& W^+,\\
 0.9975\pm0.0056,& W^-,
 \end{cases}\\
 \left.\frac{\Pi}{S}\right|_{\geq2b}
 &=\begin{cases}
 0.9634\pm0.0057,& W^+,\\
 0.9622\pm0.0055,& W^-.
 \end{cases}
\end{align}
The one-b change is consistent with zero at this precision. The two-b
cross sections decrease by about 3.7\% for both charges, and the absolute
changes exceed six estimated Monte Carlo errors. This is a statement
about numerical resolution in the present ensembles, not a Gaussian
discovery significance. The additional b-jet requirement is therefore
central to the observed difference between prescriptions.

The decay-only product changes are much smaller in the same two-b region:
$\Pi_D-D=-0.0022\pm0.0025$ fb for W$^+$ and
$-0.0013\pm0.0013$ fb for W$^-$. The corresponding contrasts involving
production corrections,
$(\Pi-S)-(\Pi_D-D)$, are $-0.0376\pm0.0068$ fb and
$-0.0204\pm0.0035$ fb. This comparison points to the importance of terms
involving the production correction. It is not an extraction of a pure
second-order coefficient: the width denominators and higher products
must be separated with the eight-subset construction described above.

Figure~\ref{fig:mainmigrations} tests the acceptance interpretation using
the same events at different jet radii and b-jet thresholds. The two-b
fraction and the extra-jet veto fraction preserve their common-rate
covariance. Their scale bands and the point-matched ratios in
Table~\ref{tab:mainresponses} quantify the response without treating the
cut choices as independent samples.
The ratio of the product to strict two-b-given-one-b efficiencies is
$0.9668\pm0.0032$ for W$^+$ and $0.9647\pm0.0029$ for W$^-$.
Normalizing to the uncut rates instead gives two-b acceptance ratios
$0.9581\pm0.0051$ and $0.9526\pm0.0045$. These definitions separate
migration between the fiducial regions from the inclusive diagnostic
discussed below.

\subsection{Stage projection from the stored scale weights}

The separate decay-scale weights permit an initial coefficient diagnostic
without additional integrations. At each fixed production-scale point
and fixed PDF member 0, define $r_i=\as(\mu_i)/\as(m_t)$. Multiplying a
product weight by its two known width ratios gives
\begin{equation}
 (1+g r_t)(1+g r_{\bar t})\,\Pi(r_t,r_{\bar t})
 = V_{00}+r_t V_{10}+r_{\bar t} V_{01}+r_t r_{\bar t}V_{11}.
\end{equation}
The analogous coefficients $U_{ij}$ from $\Pi_D$ retain Born production.
Thus $U_{00}$ is the all-Born numerator, $V_{00}-U_{00}$ contains the
production correction with Born decays, and analogous differences isolate
the other production-corrected subsets. These coefficients use the common
LO-width denominator. Production couplings and PDFs are held fixed during
this algebra; no physical strong-coupling parameter scan is used.

We determine the coefficients from the four outer decay-scale corners
within each complete run and check the other five points. Across all nine
production points and original bins, the product and strict linear
projections close to within $9.2\times10^{-11}$ pb, consistent with the
precision of the stored histograms. Errors are computed after forming the
terms within each independent ensemble, retaining their common-run
covariance. This check validates a reconstruction of the stored weights;
an independent eight-subset export remains a separate validation.

At the nominal two-b cuts, the part of $[\Pi-S]_2$ involving the production
correction, including its width counterterms, is
$-0.0367\pm0.0029$ fb for W$^+$ and $-0.01934\pm0.00125$ fb for W$^-$.
The remaining decay and width contribution is
$-0.00490\pm0.00010$ fb and $-0.002451\pm0.000061$ fb.
Their sums are $-0.0416\pm0.0029$ fb and $-0.02179\pm0.00125$ fb.
These terms identify production--decay mixing as the dominant part of the
selected second-order rate correction. The decay contribution includes
terms generated by expanding the width denominators; it is not just the
product of the two first-order normalized decay densities.

The exact product minus the reconstructed strict result gives
$-0.0450\pm0.0021$ fb and $-0.02360\pm0.00129$ fb in this region.
The reconstructed and directly integrated strict rates differ by only
0.80 and 0.59 estimated errors, with their shared-product covariance retained
when comparing shifts. We keep the direct six-prescription results as the
main comparison. The projection neither supplies missing same-stage
second-order corrections nor resolves the inclusive convergence flag,
and its PDF-member and differential attribution remain incomplete.

\subsection{Scale response and differential structure}

Figure~\ref{fig:mainscales} separates production, individual-decay and
combined scale responses. Table~\ref{tab:mainresponses} also gives the
scale envelope of $\Pi/S$ itself and its separate PDF uncertainty.
The intervals are formed after matching all physical scale coordinates;
dividing independently obtained numerator and denominator envelopes
would answer a different question. A common-scale variation can hide
compensating production and decay responses, and is displayed alongside
the independent 63-point envelope.
For the nominal two-b selections the 63-point envelopes of $\Pi/S$ are
$[0.9499,0.9772]$ for W$^+$ and $[0.9488,0.9757]$ for W$^-$. Thus the
central prediction remains below unity over this scale grid. The separate
PDF errors on these ratios are 0.00019 and 0.00029; they do not replace
the considerably larger Monte Carlo uncertainty.
For the absolute two-b cross sections, the independent 63-point scale
envelope is wider for the product prescription: $[-10.8,+10.6]\%$ versus
$[-9.5,+9.0]\%$ for W$^+$, and $[-11.3,+11.5]\%$ versus
$[-10.0,+9.9]\%$ for W$^-$. These results therefore provide no evidence
for a reduction of scale uncertainty from including the selected products.

Figures~\ref{fig:maindecay}--\ref{fig:mainangles} compare normalized shapes.
For the subleading-b 60--80 GeV bin the normalized $\Pi/S$ ratios are
$0.9508\pm0.0122$ and $0.9457\pm0.0142$ for W$^+$ and W$^-$.
In the minimax bin spanning 150--160 GeV, which contains the on-shell

These local comparisons exhibit a larger change near the endpoint than
in the integrated two-b rate. Their correlated binwise errors are not a
global shape test. Finite W widths, reconstruction through jets and the
missing same-stage corrections are particularly relevant to interpreting
this region; the on-shell result alone cannot settle that interpretation.

The event-activity and lepton-angle distributions provide complementary
controls. Their displayed errors and bands retain normalization
correlations, and the lower angular panels compare the two charges after
normalizing each separately. The extra-jet rates in
Figure~\ref{fig:mainjets} are reported absolutely in sectors where strict
NLO vanishes. The nonzero product prediction there contains selected
emissions from different stages, with the limitations of
Table~\ref{tab:jets}.

\subsection{Convergence and inclusive normalization}

The complete-run analysis exposes an inclusive-normalization diagnostic
that must be resolved before a final precision claim. The nominal uncut
$\Pi/S$ ratios are $1.0055\pm0.0026$ and $1.0101\pm0.0028$ for W$^+$ and
W$^-$. These ratios should approach unity for fully integrated, consistently
normalized on-shell decays. The W$^-$ difference $\Pi-S$ is 3.60 estimated
Monte Carlo errors. This residual is not interpreted as a physical
inclusive decay correction. The independent P and LO controls, the
paired decay-scale cancellations and the retraining influence diagnostics
are retained to distinguish normalization errors from integration effects.
In the sum of the two charges, the inclusive S--P residual is
$-0.04673\pm0.01214$ fb, or 3.85 estimated errors; D--LO is
$-0.02287\pm0.00736$ fb, or 3.11 errors. By contrast, the 576 paired
inclusive decay-scale contrasts have maximum absolute residual 2.69 errors.
The binwise S--P--D+LO check has 20 nominal entries above three errors
among 5660 nonzero-error entries, with maxima 4.29 and 3.41 for the two
charges. These entries span correlated observables and cuts and do not
constitute a global goodness-of-fit test.
Deleting any one of the 160 S/P integrations in turn leaves the
charge-summed inclusive residual between 3.61 and 4.24 estimated errors.
The corresponding diagnostic for the two-b product shift remains above
5.94 errors for W$^+$ and 6.21 for W$^-$. These deletions are influence
checks only; all runs enter the quoted predictions and errors.

The strict scale projection helps localize this residual. The integrated
decay-dependent contribution within S is
$0.00013\pm0.00055$ fb for W$^+$ and
$-0.00074\pm0.00039$ fb for W$^-$. Subtracting it leaves differences from
the independent P controls of $-0.03185\pm0.01075$ fb and
$-0.01427\pm0.00529$ fb. Most of the inclusive S--P residual therefore
lies in the decay-independent contribution, rather than in the integrated
first-order decay correction. This focuses the statistical follow-up;
it does not establish the absence of integration bias.

The statistical error target for nominal fiducial rates has been reached,
but it is a different requirement from reliable coverage of adaptive
integrations and rare tails. No error inflation, removal of inconvenient
runs or inclusive rescaling is applied. Further fixed-statistics and
retraining checks must assess the inclusive residual, while the broad
shape-error range constrains which differential conclusions can be retained.

\subsection{Finite-W-width and bottom-mass robustness}

The existing companion pilots cover all three W treatments and both
bottom-mass choices for a single ordered $e,e,\mu$ assignment per charge.
Figure~\ref{fig:wtreatmentpilot} separates the changes of the top-W and
associated-W treatments in $\Pi/S$. Their shared-control covariance is
retained; the intermediate top-BW state enters the two adjacent changes
with opposite signs. At the available conditional precision these pilots
do not resolve a W-treatment dependence of the product ratio. They cannot
be substituted for a full flavour sum.

\centering
\includegraphics[width=\linewidth]{../figures/w_treatment_pilot_width_mass_v2.pdf}
\caption{Pilot W-treatment changes of the product/strict ratio for one
ordered $e,e,\mu$ assignment in each charge. Bars are conditional Monte
Carlo errors, and comparisons share control samples. These are auxiliary
validation results, not the completed 480-run full-flavour prediction.}
\label{fig:wtreatmentpilot}
\end{figure}

The prepared full-flavour companion campaign adds 640 S/$\Pi$
integrations in massless top-BW/all-BW and massive on-shell/all-BW states,
with five retrainings per flavour and charge. It reuses the 160 main
on-shell S/$\Pi$ integrations as shared references. Its launch requires
assessment of the main normalization and convergence flags and the
narrow-W and small-mass controls. The selected PDF-family, strong-coupling,
top-mass and 13.6 TeV comparisons, and independent validation and differential
extension of the stage attribution, remain outstanding. The present numbers establish the
on-shell numerical pattern and the remaining precision questions; they do
not complete the proposed phenomenological robustness study.
