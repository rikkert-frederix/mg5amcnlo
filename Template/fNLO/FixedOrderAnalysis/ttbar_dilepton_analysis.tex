\begin{abstract}
This document specifies the comprehensive HwU analysis
\code{analysis_HwU_pp_ttx_dilepton.o} for
\(
 pp\to\ttbar+X\to b\lplus\nu_\ell\,\bar b\lminus\bar\nu_\ell+X
\)
at the LHC.  Its 247 histograms cover inclusive truth information, charged
leptons, neutrinos, reconstructed \(W\) bosons and top quarks, FastJet jet
activity, a detector-motivated fiducial region, transverse variables that do
not require the individual neutrino momenta, and the complete set of single-
and double-spin angular moments in the \(k,r,n\) basis.  The same IR-safe
measurement function is used for additive and multiplicative NLO decay-chain
events and for all real-event counterevents.
\end{abstract}

\section{Scope and intended use}

The analysis accepts one final-state positive charged lepton, one negative
charged lepton, their flavour-matched neutrino and antineutrino, one \(b\), and
one \(\bar b\).  Here \(\ell=e,\mu,\tau\); particle identification is by PDG
code and status rather than by position in the process definition.  The
positive-lepton chain is always associated with the top decay and the
negative-lepton chain with the antitop decay.  Extra final-state light quarks
and gluons are allowed.  Events that do not have a unique required set of
decay products stop with an explicit error instead of silently filling an
incorrect observable.

There are three complementary levels:

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.8em]
\item \textbf{Inclusive truth} uses the exact neutrino momenta and perfect
  identification of decay products, with no acceptance cuts.  It is useful
  for theory comparisons, spin-density studies, and understanding decay
  dynamics, but is not directly measurable.
\item \textbf{Fiducial visible} applies lepton, jet, isolation, dilepton-mass,
  and missing-momentum requirements.  It never uses the individual true
  neutrino momenta.  These observables are closest to stable-particle or
  detector-level measurements, subject to the parton-level limitations in
  Sec.~\ref{sec:limitations}.
\item \textbf{Neutrino-blind reconstructed} starts from the fiducial objects
  and total missing transverse momentum.  A deterministic mass-constraint
  fit supplies two neutrino four-momenta, reconstructed \(W^\pm\), top and
  antitop candidates, and reconstructed spin angles.  This is a transparent
  fixed-order proxy, not an emulation of a particular experimental fit.
\end{enumerate}

All plots are filled with every active HwU weight.  Thus the central value,
scale variations, PDF variations, and any other weights made available by the
run are propagated consistently.  Histogram integrals have the normal
fixed-order cross-section normalization; no shape normalization is applied by
the analysis.

\section{Kinematic conventions}

Four-vectors are \(p=(E,p_x,p_y,p_z)\), with
\begin{align}
 \pt(p)&=\sqrt{p_x^2+p_y^2}, &
 y(p)&=\frac12\ln\frac{E+p_z}{E-p_z},\\
 \eta(p)&=\frac12\ln\frac{|\vec p|+p_z}{|\vec p|-p_z}, &
 m(p)&=\sqrt{\max(0,p^2)}.
\end{align}
The unsigned azimuthal separation lies in \([0,\pi]\), while
\(\ddphi_{\rm signed}(a,b)=\operatorname{atan2}[\sin(\phi_a-\phi_b),
\cos(\phi_a-\phi_b)]\).  Angular distance is
\(\dr=\sqrt{(\Delta\eta)^2+(\ddphi)^2}\).  ``Leading'' and ``trailing''
mean largest and smallest \(\pt\), respectively.

The total missing transverse vector is constructed only from visible
final-state particles,
\begin{equation}
 \vec p_{\mathrm T}^{\,\mathrm{miss}}
 =-\sum_{i\notin\{\nu_e,\nu_\mu,\nu_\tau\}}\vec p_{\mathrm T,i}.
\end{equation}
Momentum conservation makes it equal to the dineutrino transverse momentum
for an ideal event, but the realistic reconstruction never reads either true
neutrino momentum separately.

For a visible system \(v\) and a massless invisible transverse momentum
\(\vec q_{\mathrm T}\),
\begin{equation}
 m_{\mathrm T}^2(v,\vec q_{\mathrm T})=m_v^2+2
 \left(E_{\mathrm T,v}|\vec q_{\mathrm T}|-
       \vec p_{\mathrm T,v}\!\cdot\!\vec q_{\mathrm T}\right),
 \qquad E_{\mathrm T,v}=\sqrt{m_v^2+\pt^2(v)}.
\end{equation}
The dilepton cluster transverse mass treats the two neutrinos as one massless
invisible system.  The stransverse mass is
\begin{equation}
 m_{\mathrm T2}(v_1,v_2)=
 \min_{\vec q_{1\mathrm T}+\vec q_{2\mathrm T}=\vec p_{\mathrm T}^{\rm miss}}
 \max\{m_{\mathrm T}(v_1,\vec q_{1\mathrm T}),
       m_{\mathrm T}(v_2,\vec q_{2\mathrm T})\}.
\end{equation}
The code evaluates this two-dimensional minimum with a coarse grid followed
by a convergent pattern search.  For \(m_{\mathrm T2}^{b\ell}\), both
lepton--b-jet pairings are minimized and the smaller result is retained.

\section{Truth resonances and IR-safe jets}

The exact \(W\) candidates are
\(p_{\Wp}=p_{\lplus}+p_\nu\) and
\(p_{\Wm}=p_{\lminus}+p_{\bar\nu}\).  At Born level the top candidates are
\(p_{\Wp}+p_b\) and \(p_{\Wm}+p_{\bar b}\).  With real radiation, every
non-bottom final-state QCD parton is assigned to production, the top decay, or
the antitop decay.  Of the \(3^{N_{\rm rad}}\) assignments, the analysis picks
the one minimizing
\begin{equation}
 \chi^2_{\rm truth}=
 \left(\frac{m_{t,\rm cand}-m_t^{\rm ref}}{5\ \mathrm{GeV}}\right)^2+
 \left(\frac{m_{\bar t,\rm cand}-m_t^{\rm ref}}{5\ \mathrm{GeV}}\right)^2.
\end{equation}
This makes the reconstruction independent of event-record ordering and keeps
decay radiation with the resonance that it reconstructs.  Soft and collinear
limits are unchanged except on measure-zero assignment boundaries.  The
reference masses are read from the model parameters.

All final-state quarks with \(|\mathrm{PDG}|\leq5\) and all gluons are passed
to FastJet.  Jets use the anti-\(k_T\) algorithm with \(R=0.4\); unless stated
otherwise, counted jets have \(\pt>25\) GeV and \(|\eta|<4.5\).  A jet is
ideally b-tagged when it contains the unique final-state \(b\) or \(\bar b\)
parton.  If both are clustered into one jet, inclusive jet plots remain
well-defined but the two-distinct-b-jet fiducial requirement fails.  Additional
jets are all accepted jets other than the two b-tagged jet indices.

Define
\begin{align}
 H_{\mathrm T}^{j}&=\sum_{j}\pt(j), &
 H_{\mathrm T}^{\rm extra}&=\sum_{j\notin b,\bar b}\pt(j),\\
 H_{\mathrm T}^{\rm vis}&=\pt(\lplus)+\pt(\lminus)+H_{\mathrm T}^{j}, &
 S_{\mathrm T}&=H_{\mathrm T}^{\rm vis}+\met,\\
 p_{\rm vis}&=p_{\lplus}+p_{\lminus}+p_{b\text{-jet}}+p_{\bar b\text{-jet}}.
\end{align}
The two jet-gap histograms are cumulative cross sections, not differential
spectra: their bin centred at \(Q\) receives an event when the leading
additional-jet \(\pt<Q\), or when \(H_{\mathrm T}^{\rm extra}<Q\),
respectively.  Threshold centres run from 15 to 205 GeV in 10 GeV steps.

\section{Fiducial region and cutflow}
\label{sec:fiducial}

The baseline fiducial selection is deliberately simple and reproducible:
\begin{align*}
 \pt(\ell_1)&>25\ \mathrm{GeV}, & \pt(\ell_2)&>20\ \mathrm{GeV},
 & |\eta(\ell)|&<2.5,\\
 m_{\ell\ell}&>20\ \mathrm{GeV}, &
 \pt(b\text{-jet})&>30\ \mathrm{GeV}, & |\eta(b\text{-jet})|&<2.5,\\
 \min_{\ell,j}\dr(\ell,j)&>0.4, & \met&>20\ \mathrm{GeV}.&&
\end{align*}
Two distinct b-tagged jets are required.  The isolation minimum includes all
jets with \(\pt>25\) GeV and \(|\eta|<4.5\).  An additional same-flavour
signal-region stage requires
\(|m_{\ell\ell}-91.1876\ \mathrm{GeV}|>15\) GeV and \(\met>40\) GeV;
different-flavour events pass this stage without the extra requirements.

The eight cutflow bin centres mean:
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{cl}
\toprule
Bin & Requirement accumulated through this bin\\
\midrule
1 & all valid dileptonic events\\
2 & lepton \(\pt\) and \(|\eta|\)\\
3 & dilepton mass\\
4 & two distinct accepted b-tagged jets\\
5 & lepton--jet separation\\
6 & baseline missing transverse momentum (the fiducial sample)\\
7 & same-flavour \(Z\)-veto and tightened \(\met\), when applicable\\
8 & successful neutrino reconstruction after bin 7\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

The visible \(b\ell\) pairing chooses between
\(\{\lplus b,\lminus\bar b\}\) and
\(\{\lplus\bar b,\lminus b\}\) by selecting the pairing with the smaller
maximum pair mass.  The minimum, maximum, and average masses in the chosen
pairing are plotted.  This definition uses no neutrino information.

\section{Neutrino-blind reconstruction}

For a trial transverse split
\(\vec q_{\mathrm T}\) and
\(\vec p_{\mathrm T}^{\rm miss}-\vec q_{\mathrm T}\), the two equations
\((p_{\ell}+p_\nu)^2=(m_W^{\rm ref})^2\) give up to two longitudinal roots per
side.  When a discriminant is negative, its real part is used.  Both roots and
both b-jet/lepton pairings are tested.  The selected solution minimizes
\begin{align}
 \chi^2_{\rm reco}={}&
 \sum_{q=t,\bar t}\left(\frac{m_q-m_t^{\rm ref}}{15\ \mathrm{GeV}}\right)^2
 +\sum_{V=\Wp,\Wm}\left(\frac{m_V-m_W^{\rm ref}}{10\ \mathrm{GeV}}\right)^2
 +\left(\frac{m_t-m_{\bar t}}{30\ \mathrm{GeV}}\right)^2.
\end{align}
The transverse split is initialized on a \(5\times5\) grid around half of
\(\vec p_{\mathrm T}^{\rm miss}\), followed by up to twelve eight-direction
pattern-search refinements.  The procedure is deterministic and uses only the
two charged leptons, two b-tagged jets, total \(\vec p_{\mathrm T}^{\rm miss}\),
and the model pole masses.  Consequently reconstructed truth-comparison plots
are validation diagnostics; all other reconstructed plots are in principle
obtainable without observing either neutrino separately.

\section{Spin-correlation observables}
\label{sec:spin}

Spin axes are constructed in the \(\ttbar\) rest frame.  Let \(\hat p\) be
the boosted positive-beam direction, \(\hat k\) the top direction, and
\(c=\hat p\cdot\hat k\).  The beam-basis triad is
\begin{equation}
 \hat r=\operatorname{sign}(c)\,
 \frac{\hat p-c\hat k}{\sqrt{1-c^2}},\qquad
 \hat n=\operatorname{sign}(c)\,
 \frac{\hat p\times\hat k}{\sqrt{1-c^2}},\qquad
 \hat k=\frac{\vec p_t^{\,\ttbar}}{|\vec p_t^{\,\ttbar}|}.
\end{equation}
The sign factors make the axes appropriate for a symmetric \(pp\) initial
state.  A deterministic orthogonal completion is used at the exceptional
forward points.  The charged leptons are boosted first into the \(\ttbar\)
frame and then into their respective parent rest frames.  With
\(a,b\in\{k,r,n\}\),
\begin{equation}
 c_a^+=\hat\ell_+\cdot\hat a,\qquad
 c_b^-=\hat\ell_-\cdot\hat b,
 \qquad q_{ab}=c_a^+c_b^-.
\end{equation}
The six one-spin angles and all nine products are histogrammed.  This is the
complete angular information needed for the single-spin polarizations and the
\(3\times3\) spin-correlation matrix.  In the commonly used convention
\begin{equation}
 \frac1\sigma\frac{d^2\sigma}{dc_a^+dc_b^-}
 =\frac14\left[1+B_a^+c_a^+ + B_b^-c_b^- -C_{ab}c_a^+c_b^-\right],
\end{equation}
the moments obey \(B_a^\pm=3\langle c_a^\pm\rangle\) and
\(C_{ab}=-9\langle q_{ab}\rangle\).  Stating this convention matters: the
histograms themselves are raw, convention-independent angular moments.

The symmetric and antisymmetric off-diagonal combinations expose
\(C_{ab}\!+C_{ba}\) and \(C_{ab}\!-C_{ba}\).  The event-level diagonal
quantity is
\(d_{\rm diag}=(q_{kk}+q_{rr}+q_{nn})/3\); with the convention above,
\(D\equiv\operatorname{tr}C/3=-9\langle d_{\rm diag}\rangle\).
The lepton opening cosine, laboratory \(\ddphi_{\ell\ell}\), signed
\(\sin\ddphi_{\ell\ell}\), and
\((\hat\ell_+\times\hat\ell_-)\cdot\hat k\) provide compact spin and
CP-sensitive projections.  The complete set is filled once with exact truth
tops (histograms 191--216) and once with reconstructed tops (217--242).

For the \(W\)-helicity angles, the charged lepton is evaluated in its \(W\)
rest frame against the direction opposite to the top-decay recoil in that
frame.  This exact definition is used for both charges and avoids ambiguity
about the anti-top decay-product ordering.

\section{Fixed-order and IR-safety considerations}

The analysis is called on real events and on their soft, collinear, and
soft-collinear projections.  It deliberately does not branch on the internal
body label.  Anti-\(k_T\) jets, inclusive missing momentum, the deterministic
minimizations, and all measurement cuts are applied identically to each
event/counterevent.  Therefore unresolved momenta that are indistinguishable
to an IR-safe observable remain indistinguishable to the analysis.  Sharp cut
boundaries and discrete assignment boundaries can differ only on sets of zero
phase-space measure, as in an ordinary fixed-order analysis.

The truth-top assignment is resonance-aware but not an event-history query.
This matters for the multiplicative path, whose combined event can contain
different real/Born momentum choices for different production and decay
blocks.  The analysis reconstructs solely from the materialized external
state, so additive and multiplicative results use the same observable
definition.

Jet multiplicities and jet vetoes are meaningful fixed-order observables, but
large logarithms can make the exclusive zero-additional-jet region sensitive
to resummation.  Similarly, tails near kinematic endpoints can carry large
scale dependence.  HwU scale/PDF variations should be inspected rather than
interpreting only a central curve.

\section{Running the analysis}

Select the module in \code{Cards/FO_analyse_card.dat}:
\begin{verbatim}
FO_ANALYSIS_FORMAT = HwU
FO_ANALYSE         = analysis_HwU_pp_ttx_dilepton.o
\end{verbatim}
The companion ABI bridge is selected automatically.  FastJet is already part
of the fixed-order template link, so no extra FastJet entry is needed in
\code{FO_EXTRALIBS}.  The module works for both
\code{NLO_DECAY_COMBINATION ADDITIVE} and
\code{NLO_DECAY_COMBINATION MULTIPLICATIVE}.  The output is
\code{MADatNLO.HwU}; standard HwU post-processing may be used for uncertainty
bands, normalized distributions, ratios, and publication plots.

For a direct-decay process, a representative syntax is
\begin{verbatim}
generate p p > t t~ [QCD], \
 (t > w+ b QED=1 [QCD], w+ > e+ ve), \
 (t~ > w- b~ QED=1 [QCD], w- > mu- vm~)
\end{verbatim}
The lepton flavour and the order of decay products in the process command do
not affect object mapping.

\section{Limitations and interpretation}
\label{sec:limitations}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.8em]
\item Jets are parton-level FastJet jets.  There is no shower,
  hadronization, pile-up, detector response, lepton dressing, or b-tagging
  efficiency/mistag model.  Ideal constituent flavour labels are used only to
  identify the two b jets.
\item The fiducial cuts are a generic LHC-like reference selection, not the
  exact selection of a specific ATLAS or CMS publication.  A comparison to
  data must reproduce that measurement's object definitions, flavour
  channels, dressing, jet acceptance, and unfolding level.
\item The neutrino reconstruction is a fast deterministic constraint fit.
  Experimental methods can include transfer functions, multiple detector
  hypotheses, likelihood weights, and resolution smearing.  Truth-residual
  plots 187--188 are not observable in data.
\item Exact neutrino, exact \(W\), truth-top, and truth-spin plots assume
  perfect knowledge and are theory-only.  Fiducial lepton, jet, missing-
  momentum, \(m_{\mathrm T2}\), and visible-system plots require much less
  reconstruction and are the preferred measurement-facing set.
\item The analysis expects exactly one final charged lepton of each sign and a
  unique flavour-matched neutrino pair.  It does not unfold secondary
  \(\tau\) decays or additional electroweak lepton pairs.
\end{itemize}

\section{Primary references}

The complete spin-density-matrix organization and the \(k,r,n\) basis follow
the framework of Bernreuther, Heisler, and Si,
\href{https://arxiv.org/abs/1508.05271}{arXiv:1508.05271}.  Experimental use
of the full set of 15 independent polarization and correlation coefficients
is illustrated by ATLAS,
\href{https://arxiv.org/abs/1612.07004}{arXiv:1612.07004}, and comprehensive
dilepton spin measurements by CMS,
\href{https://arxiv.org/abs/1907.03729}{arXiv:1907.03729}.

Representative LHC differential measurements motivating the top, lepton,
dilepton, b-jet, and additional-jet catalogue include CMS
\href{https://arxiv.org/abs/1708.07638}{arXiv:1708.07638} and
\href{https://arxiv.org/abs/1811.06625}{arXiv:1811.06625}, and ATLAS
\href{https://arxiv.org/abs/1709.09407}{arXiv:1709.09407},
\href{https://arxiv.org/abs/1606.09490}{arXiv:1606.09490}, and
\href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.15340}{arXiv:2303.15340}.  Dineutrino and
missing-momentum-sensitive observables are discussed in the recent CMS study
\href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.00160}{arXiv:2510.00160}; the visible
\(m_{b\ell}\) endpoint is used, for example, in the ATLAS top-mass analysis
\href{https://arxiv.org/abs/1503.05427}{arXiv:1503.05427}.

Jet finding uses FastJet
\href{https://arxiv.org/abs/1111.6097}{arXiv:1111.6097} and the anti-\(k_T\)
algorithm of Cacciari, Salam, and Soyez,
\href{https://arxiv.org/abs/0802.1189}{arXiv:0802.1189}.
