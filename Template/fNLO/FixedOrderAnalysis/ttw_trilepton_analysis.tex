\begin{abstract}
The HwU analysis \code{analysis_HwU_pp_ttxw_trilepton.o} contains
\nplots{} histograms for the $2e1\mu+3\nu+b\bar b$ final state.
It retains inclusive truth observables and supplies \fidstride{} fiducial
observables in each of the exclusive zero-, one-, and two-bottom-jet categories.
The fiducial observables use charged leptons, recombined jets, and total
missing transverse momentum, without fitting the neutrinos or reconstructing
top and W candidates from missing momentum. All active weights are filled
with the same measurement function for additive and multiplicative events
and their subtraction counterevents.
\end{abstract}

\section{Event content and interfaces}
The analysis accepts two status-one electrons or positrons, one muon of
either charge, three light neutrinos, one bottom parton and one antibottom
parton, together with additional QCD radiation. The charged-lepton total
charge is $q_{3\ell}=\pm1$, with two same-sign leptons and one opposite-sign
lepton. Both $e^+e^-\mu^\pm$ and $e^\pm e^\pm\mu^\mp$ patterns are supported.
Identification uses PDG codes, not record position or parent ancestry.
Electron and positron distributions always refer to their explicit charges;
a two-electron event of one charge contributes twice to that species'
single-particle distribution. Muon distributions include both charges, with
additional charge-separated muon plots.

The module and its matching ABI bridge provide \code{analysis_begin},
\code{analysis_end}, \code{analysis_fill}, and the variable-particle-count
\code{analysis_fill_multiplicative} entry point. The latter accommodates
simultaneous production and decay radiation. Select the analysis with
\begin{verbatim}
FO_ANALYSIS_FORMAT=HwU
FO_ANALYSE=analysis_HwU_pp_ttxw_trilepton.o
\end{verbatim}
The launcher automatically adds its matching bridge.

\section{Common fiducial selection}
The three exclusive categories share the following lepton selection:
\begin{itemize}
\item each charged lepton has $\pt\geq20$ GeV and $|\eta|<2.5$,
      independently of its flavour and charge;
\item when the electron pair is opposite sign, $m_{ee}>12$ GeV;
\item every selected lepton is separated from every accepted central jet by
\[
 \Delta R(\ell,j)\geq
 \min\left(0.4,\;0.04+\frac{10\,\mathrm{GeV}}{\pt(\ell)}\right).
\]
\end{itemize}
There are no Z-mass vetoes on the dilepton or three-lepton invariant mass.
There is no minimum number of jets or tagged jets in the common selection,
and no missing-transverse-momentum cut. In particular, an event with no
accepted jets can populate the zero-bottom-jet category. The separation
criterion is a specified partonic isolation proxy, not a detector simulation.

All status-one QCD partons are clustered with anti-$k_T$, $R=0.4$, and
four-vector recombination. Accepted jets have $\pt\geq25$ GeV and
$|\eta|<2.5$. A bottom jet contains the unique bottom parton and not the
antibottom parton; an antibottom jet contains the unique antibottom parton
and not the bottom parton. A jet containing both is excluded from the
bottom-tag count and all bottom/antibottom-specific distributions. It remains
an ordinary jet for jet counts, scalar jet activity and lepton--jet separation.
These definitions are appropriate to the supported fixed-order final state,
which has one primary bottom and one primary antibottom; they do not define
an arbitrary-multiplicity flavour algorithm for additional $b\bar b$ pairs.

The accepted tag count $N_b$ is exactly zero, one, or two. Each event passing
the common selection fills precisely one of the three rate histograms,
\code{fiducial 0b rate}, \code{fiducial 1b rate}, or \code{fiducial 2b rate}.
The categories are exclusive, not ``at least'' requirements. A species or
pair observable is filled only if its required accepted objects exist;
missing objects are not represented by zero momentum. Thus the zero-tag
category has lepton and event observables but no tagged-jet entries. In the
one-tag category only the accepted tag's species and combinations are filled.

\section{Generation cuts in the current study exports}
The current \code{PROC_fNLO_loop_sm-no_b_mass_2} and
\code{PROC_fNLO_loop_sm-no_b_mass_3} exports also apply the common fiducial
baseline in \code{SubProcesses/cuts.f90}. The public analysis helper
\code{passes_fiducial_baseline} reuses the lepton acceptance, low-mass
threshold and jet-isolation selection above. It imposes no bottom-tag count
or jet-multiplicity requirement. Bottom jets participate in lepton isolation
as ordinary accepted jets, including jets containing both bottom flavours.

The helper is called after the longitudinal boost to the lab and before the
decay-product mask for generic run-card cuts. Consequently the custom baseline
remains active with \code{cut_decays=False}. It is evaluated separately for
each real event and subtraction counterevent, including variable-size
multiplicative events; a failing Born projection does not veto a passing real
configuration. The process makefiles make \code{cuts.o} depend on the analysis
module. These are process-local changes: a fresh export needs the same call
and dependency added to enable this generation selection.

With these generation cuts, all histograms, including those labelled
\code{inclusive} or \code{truth}, are conditional on passing the common
fiducial baseline. Their rates and cutflows therefore differ from runs
integrated without that baseline. The three exclusive category rates still
sum to the selected total. New run names must be used for this selection.

\section{Fiducial observables}
Each category has the same booked observable catalogue and its own
\code{fiducial 0b}, \code{fiducial 1b}, or \code{fiducial 2b} title prefix.
\begin{longtable}{@{}p{0.30\linewidth}p{0.65\linewidth}@{}}
\toprule Objects & Observables\\ \midrule \endhead
Electrons, positrons, muons; also $\mu^+$ and $\mu^-$ separately &
$\pt$, pseudorapidity $\eta$, rapidity $y$, azimuth $\phi$, energy $E$.\\
Accepted bottom and antibottom jets separately &
$\pt$, $\eta$, $y$, $\phi$, $E$, invariant jet mass.\\
Charged-lepton pairs $e^-e^+$, $e^-e^-$, $e^+e^+$, $e^-\mu$, $e^+\mu$ &
Invariant mass, pair $\pt$, pair rapidity, $\Delta R$, $|\Delta\phi|$,
$|\Delta\eta|$. Each unordered physical pair is filled once.\\
Same-sign pair and leading opposite-sign pair &
The same six pair observables; the leading opposite-sign pair combines the
unique opposite-sign lepton with the highest-$\pt$ same-sign lepton.\\
Electron, positron, or muon with either accepted tagged-jet species &
The same six pair observables, for all six species combinations.\\
Accepted bottom--antibottom jet pair &
The same six pair observables, only in the two-tag category.\\
Three-lepton system &
Invariant mass, transverse momentum, rapidity and scalar lepton
$H_{\mathrm T}$.\\
Visible system and event activity &
Visible combinations use the three charged leptons and accepted tagged jets;
jet activity counts all accepted jets. The catalogue includes visible mass
and transverse momentum, scalar activity, $S_{\mathrm T}$ including missing
momentum, missing transverse momentum, accepted-jet multiplicity and scalar
jet $H_{\mathrm T}$, and ordered lepton transverse momenta.\\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}

For objects $a,b$, pair quantities are evaluated on $p_a+p_b$;
$\Delta R=\sqrt{(\eta_a-\eta_b)^2+\Delta\phi^2}$. Four-vectors use
$(E,p_x,p_y,p_z)$, and $m=\sqrt{\max(0,p^2)}$. All energies and momenta
are in GeV. The missing vector is calculated from all visible status-one
particles,
\[
 \vec p_{\mathrm T}^{\,\mathrm{miss}}=
 -\sum_{i\notin\{\nu_e,\nu_\mu,\nu_\tau\}}\vec p_{\mathrm T,i},
\]
including unaccepted radiation. Individual neutrino momenta are not used
for any fiducial observable. The visible-system scalar sum and scalar jet
sum have distinct titles so accepted light-jet activity is explicit.

\section{Retained inclusive truth layer}
The truth interpretation uses $e^+\nu_e$ for the top-side $W^+$,
$e^-\bar\nu_e$ for the antitop-side $W^-$, and the muon and its
charge-compatible neutrino for the associated W. Truth-only resonance,
polarization and spin observables are filled only if this unique PDG content
is present. The fiducial layer still fills for alternative parent-origin
assignments and same-sign electrons.

Truth top momenta include decay radiation, identified by minimizing the
sum of squared top/antitop mass residuals over the production/top/antitop
assignment of each extra QCD parton. This uses exact neutrino momenta and
is separate from the removed missing-momentum reconstruction. The retained
lepton--bottom masses use recombined bottom jets rather than bare massless
bottom partons. Merged bottom--antibottom jets do not fill tagged-jet or
lepton--tag observables. The unresolvable assigned-parton-count histogram
has been removed. Exact angular upper endpoints are placed in the final
physical bin, including the Born back-to-back $\Delta\phi(W,t\bar t)=\pi$.

The truth spin basis is defined in the $t\bar t$ rest frame using the boosted
positive beam $\hat p$ and top direction $\hat k$. With $c=\hat p\cdot\hat k$,
\[
 \hat r=\operatorname{sign}(c)\frac{\hat p-c\hat k}{\sqrt{1-c^2}},\qquad
 \hat n=\operatorname{sign}(c)\frac{\hat p\times\hat k}{\sqrt{1-c^2}}.
\]
The charged leptons in their parent rest frames supply six spin cosines,
their nine products, and additional opening-angle and triple-product
observables. Associated-W polarization and associated-lepton spin
observables are retained at truth level only. The associated-W helicity axis
is the negative $t\bar t$ recoil direction in the W rest frame; the
charged-lepton analyser is boosted to that same frame.

The cutflow records valid object content, lepton acceptance, opposite-sign
same-flavour mass threshold, and lepton--jet separation.
Its last bin is the common fiducial baseline and
has central value equal to the sum of the three exclusive category rates.

Histogram weights are signed fixed-order cross sections in pb per bin,
not automatically divided by bin widths. All configured central, scale and
PDF weights are filled. Object and pair histograms count their entries:
when a species occurs twice, its integral can be twice the category rate.
Only the category rate counts every accepted event exactly once. Truncated
plot ranges can also limit histogram integrals. Bin errors include
correlated subtraction contributions within an integration point; covariance
between different bins must be retained separately for rebinned or
normalized-shape error estimates.

The catalogue and fiducial definition changed on 15 October 2026. Old
286-histogram files contain the retired neutrino-fit observables and use
different histogram numbering and cuts. They cannot be relabelled or
recombined with the new histograms. Fresh integration output is required.
On 19 October 2026 the lepton acceptance was changed to the common
$\pt\geq20$ GeV and $|\eta|<2.5$ requirements and both Z-mass vetoes were
removed. The cutflow now has four bins; the observable catalogue and its
581 histogram IDs are unchanged. Earlier output retains its original cuts
and must not be combined with output using this selection.
